ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Making Data Easier to Understand

Student Analytics is a web-based data analysis platform designed to transform raw datasets into meaningful information through dashboards and visualizations.

OUR PURPOSE

From Raw Data to Meaningful Insights

Data can contain hundreds or thousands of records, making it difficult to identify important information by simply looking at rows and columns.

Student Analytics provides a visual environment where users can upload datasets, explore information, view key statistics and create charts based on their requirements.

Although the platform is suitable for student datasets, the Chart Builder is flexible enough to work with other structured datasets such as sales and business data.

CSV Supported
XLSX Supported
4+ Chart Types
100% Interactive
🎯

Our Vision

Our goal is to make data analysis accessible to users without requiring complicated analytical tools. The platform focuses on simplicity, visualization and practical understanding.

💡

Our Approach

Instead of presenting data only as rows and columns, Student Analytics helps users transform information into KPIs, charts and dashboards that are easier to read and interpret.
